# Week 1 Campaign Starter — Fictional local café

**Assumption:** fictional café “Bean & Bloom,” serving students near a college. Replace with the assigned business if provided.

## Persona

**Aarav, 20 — student:** studies nearby and wants an affordable, quiet place to meet friends. Finds options through Maps and Instagram; worries about price, Wi-Fi, and crowding.

**Nisha, 29 — hybrid worker:** works from cafés several days a week and needs reliable Wi-Fi, outlets, clear opening hours, and a comfortable seat. Finds options through local search and reviews; compares noise, seating, and weekday offers before visiting.

## 7-day content calendar

Mon: menu carousel; Tue: short barista intro; Wed: study-space photo; Thu: student poll; Fri: featured drink; Sat: customer-submitted moment (with consent); Sun: hours and weekly offer. Track reach, saves, profile actions, and direction taps.

## Starter strategy

Use consistent location and menu information, accessible captions, and a simple offer page. Measure weekly and compare against a baseline before claiming campaign lift. This is a fictional exercise, not a live campaign.
