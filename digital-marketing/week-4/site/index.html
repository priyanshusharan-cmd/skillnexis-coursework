Frequently asked questions

Is Hisaab free?

Yes. Adding expenses, splitting and settling are free with no daily limits. We don't charge for payments because Hisaab never handles the money; you pay through your own UPI app.

Does Hisaab hold my money or see my UPI PIN?

No. Settling opens your UPI app with the amount filled in. The payment goes bank to bank, and your PIN is entered only in your UPI app.

Can my friends use it without installing the app?

Yes. Share a WhatsApp link; friends can view the group, add expenses and pay from the browser.

How is it different from Google Pay's split?

Google Pay splits one bill at a time. Hisaab keeps a running balance across every expense in a group: a whole semester of room costs or a whole trip.

Can I split unequally?

Yes: equally, by exact amounts, by shares, or by percentage. Untick anyone who wasn't there.
