# Email sequence: waitlist → launch → activation (6 emails)

Templates: reuse the responsive layout from [week 2](../../week-2/email/) with Hisaab colours. Every email has one CTA, plain-text alt, unsubscribe link.

| # | Trigger / timing | Subject | Preview text | CTA | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Joins waitlist (instant) | You're on the list, founding roommate 🏠 | Here's your badge, and how to move up the list. | Invite your roommates | Confirm + referral (each invite moves you up) |
| 2 | Day 3 | The 5 fairest ways to split hostel costs | A 2-minute guide your room will actually use. | Read the guide | Nurture with value (links to the blog, SEO) |
| 3 | Launch day | Hisaab is live, your early access is ready | Download, create your room, add the first expense. | Get it on Google Play | Install |
| 4 | Installed, no group in 24 h | Your first group takes 30 seconds | Name it, add your roommates from WhatsApp, done. | Create your group | Activation |
| 5 | Day 7 after install, has a group | Settle up without the awkward chat | Pay anyone in your group with any UPI app, in one tap. | Settle a balance | Core action |
| 6 | 30 days inactive | Your room's balance is waiting | {{owed_or_owe_line}}. One tap to see who owes what. | Open Hisaab | Re-engage; stop after this one if there's no response |

**Measured by:** open rate (directional only), click rate, installs per email (UTM `utm_source=email&utm_campaign=launch&utm_content=e{n}`), activation rate (first expense within 24 h), unsubscribes below 0.5 %.
