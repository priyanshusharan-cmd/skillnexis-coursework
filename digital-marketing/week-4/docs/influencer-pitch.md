# Influencer outreach pitch

**Target creators:** college-life and hostel-humour creators on Instagram and YouTube Shorts, with 20k–150k followers and an audience mostly 18–24 in Indian metros and college towns. Micro-creators have higher engagement and are affordable, and their audience matches the room-sharing problem.
Shortlist criteria: engagement rate above 3 %, authentic comments (not bots), no recent fintech or betting promos, content shot in hostels or PGs.

## DM / email (≈ 120 words)
**Subject:** Collab idea: "who owes whom" in a real hostel room (paid)

Hi {{name}},
Your "{{specific video}}" had my whole room in splits: the "bhai ₹50 de dena" bit is painfully real.

I'm with **Hisaab**, a free app that tracks shared expenses for roommates and settles them in one tap with UPI. We never hold money and there are no ads.

**The idea:** give you and your roommates Hisaab for 2 weeks, then a Reel revealing who owed whom (and who "forgot"). Your script and your humour; we just need an honest take.

**What we offer:** ₹{{fee}} + a free month of pizza for your room, plus your own invite code that earns a "Founding roommate" badge for your followers.

Open to a 10-minute call this week?
{{sender}}, Hisaab

## Brief (sent if they say yes)
| | |
|---|---|
| Deliverables | 1 Reel (30–60 s) + 3 stories (one with the link sticker); usage rights 3 months for paid ads (whitelisting) |
| Must include | That it's free; "we never hold your money"; the invite code; **#ad / Paid partnership label** (ASCI influencer guidelines) |
| Avoid | Claims we can't back (e.g. "most secure app"); showing real UPI IDs or balances of people who haven't consented |
| Timeline | Brief → draft in 7 days → one round of feedback → post in launch week |
| Measured by | Views, saves, sticker taps, sign-ups with their code, cost per sign-up vs paid ads |

## Affiliate option
For smaller creators (5k–20k): no fee, ₹10 per verified sign-up with their code (capped at ₹5,000), paid monthly.
