# Plan: +50 followers in 2 weeks, organically

**What has to happen on a real account:** creating the Instagram page and posting has to be done by you, on your account,
because it involves signing up and publishing publicly. Everything to run it is ready: the assets, captions and
calendar, and the follower log in [engagement-tracker.xlsx](engagement-tracker.xlsx) (sheet *Followers*). No
results are claimed here until the log is filled in.

## Set-up (day 0, 30 minutes)
1. **Profile:** name "Bean & Bloom · Study Café"; bio "☕ Study-friendly café near campus · 📶 fast Wi-Fi · 🔌 plugs at every seat · Study Fuel ₹149, 3–7 pm"; link to the Maps listing; location set.
2. Pin 3 posts: the launch (01), the offer (02) and the study spots carousel (06).
3. Story highlights: Menu · Study spots · Offers · Hours (use story assets 12–15).
4. Record today's follower count in the *Followers* sheet as the baseline.

## Daily routine (20 minutes a day)
| When | Action | Why it brings followers |
|---|---|---|
| Within 1 h of posting | Reply to every comment and DM | Early replies keep a post in front of more people |
| 1 pm & 8 pm | Post per the [calendar](content-calendar-2wk.md); stories daily | Students' peak times |
| 10 min | Comment genuinely (not "nice pic") on 10 posts from local student pages, college clubs and Bengaluru food pages | Shows up in their followers' feeds |
| 5 min | Reshare (with permission) anyone who tags the café or uses #StudyFuelCorner | Their friends see it and follow |

## Offline → online (the biggest lever for a café)
* QR table tents: "Follow for the Study Fuel code". The follow happens while the customer is sitting there.
* At the counter: "Following us? Show it and the Study Streak card gets a bonus stamp."
* Ask 2–3 college clubs to hold one meeting at the big table; tag them in a story and they repost.

## Collaborations (week 2)
* Joint story or reel with one student creator or club page (offer a free combo for their meeting, no payment).
* Instagram Collab post with a nearby stationery or bookshop: "exam survival kit" giveaway, entry = follow both + tag a friend.

## Targets and checks
| Day | Expected cumulative followers | If behind |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | +10 | Put up the table tents (offline is the fastest) |
| 7 | +25 | Run the collab giveaway earlier |
| 14 | **+50** | Review which posts drove profile visits (tracker) and repeat that format |

**Avoid:** buying followers, follow-for-follow, engagement pods. They inflate the number but not visits, and Instagram limits reach for them.
