# Monetization plan: free to play

**Status: a plan only.** The game contains no ads, purchases or payment code, and none should be added without the store accounts, a privacy policy and the age-rating answers below.

## Principles
1. **Never sell winning.** Nothing sold makes the player stronger or the game easier to finish. All three levels and every enemy and hazard stay free.
2. **The player chooses.** No ads interrupt play, appear on launch, cover buttons, or appear after a game over without being asked for.
3. **No pressure tactics.** No fake countdowns, no loot boxes, no resource that runs out and must be refilled with money or ads.
4. **A fair, short free game.** Players should be able to judge the game completely before spending anything.

## What would be offered
| Offer | Type | Price | Why it is fair |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Cosmetic skins** for the player character (colors and hats) | One-time in-app purchase | Low, a few tiers | Changes appearance only. No effect on physics, health or score. |
| **Level pack** (new levels added after launch) | One-time in-app purchase, or free updates to start | Medium | The base three levels remain free. Extra content is optional and shown honestly as extra. |
| **Remove ads** (only if ads are ever added) | One-time purchase | Low | Gives ad-free play without making the game easier. |
| **Optional rewarded video** to continue after a game over | Opt-in ad | Free | Offered as a button the player can ignore. Retrying from the last checkpoint is always free, so declining costs only a restart that is free anyway. Capped at one offer per level attempt. |

## How the player experience is protected
- No ad or offer during active play, on the first launch, or before the player has finished level 1.
- Prices are shown before any purchase, with a confirmation step, and purchases can be restored.
- No dark patterns: no pre-selected purchase, no "no thanks" shown as shameful, no hidden close buttons.
- The game economy has **no currency**, so there is nothing to inflate, grind or lock behind payment. Difficulty comes only from the level design.
- Data collected is minimal (`analytics-plan.md`), and a "Delete my saved data" button already exists in the menu.

## Game economy and difficulty
Score comes from coins (10), stomps (25) and a clear-time and health bonus. It is a result, not a currency. The difficulty curve is the three levels in order, Meadow, then Caverns with spikes and a wider pit, then Summit with the tightest jumps. There is no way to buy past a level, so it cannot become pay to win.

## Compliance to settle before any money is involved
- **Age and audience:** if the game is aimed at children, ad networks and purchases face strict rules (for example COPPA in the US and the Designed for Families programme on Google Play). Safest default: no ads and no purchases for a children's audience.
- **Privacy policy** and the stores' data-safety and privacy-label forms.
- **Store billing** must be used for digital goods (Google Play Billing, Apple In-App Purchase).
- **Taxes and payouts** need a merchant account.

## Success measures, and what would change the plan
- Share of sessions with a purchase, and refund requests (if refunds are high, the offer is unclear).
- Ratings and review text mentioning ads or pricing (any "pay to win" complaint means something went wrong).
- Retention. If monetization lowers it, remove the offer that caused the drop.
