## Full description
Gem Run is a fast, friendly platformer. Run and jump through three levels (Meadow, Caverns and Summit), collect coins, stomp patrolling enemies, dodge spikes and pits, and reach the flag.

- Three hand-built levels, each a little harder than the last
- Checkpoints so a mistake never costs the whole level
- Simple controls: left, right and jump, with big touch buttons on phones and tablets
- Plays offline once installed
- Saves your progress and best scores on your device
- Volume and mute controls, and a button to delete your saved data any time

No ads, no purchases, no account, and nothing leaves your device.
