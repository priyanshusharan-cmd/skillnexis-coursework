# Educational Content Creator — Week 1

A starter channel plan for short beginner-friendly programming lessons. Audience profiles and topic demand are hypotheses for planning; no analytics account or audience interviews were supplied. Validate search interest and competitor performance before publishing.

- `channel-plan.md`: niche, positioning, two audience personas, competitor review framework, and a month calendar.
- `scripts.md`: three structured scripts designed for roughly five minutes each.
- `topic-pipeline.csv`: ten candidate topics with search intent and a validation step.

The scripts are production-ready drafts. They have not been recorded or published.
