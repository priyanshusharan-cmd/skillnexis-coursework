# Account, emails and privacy
To stop course emails, click "unsubscribe" at the bottom of any email or reply with the word unsubscribe.

To delete your data or change your email address, ask the support team: requests are handled by a person within 2 working days. Never share your password with the bot or the support team; we will never ask for it.

If you forgot your password, use "Forgot password" on the sign-in page to get a reset link by email.
