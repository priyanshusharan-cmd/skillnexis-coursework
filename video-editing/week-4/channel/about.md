## About section (YouTube allows 1,000 characters; this text is 642)
Room 214 is about student money, without the awkwardness.

I'm Priyanshu, a student who got tired of hostel-room arguments about who paid for the Wi-Fi. Every Sunday I post one
video about splitting expenses, settling up over UPI, and the money habits that keep friendships intact. I also share
my progress designing Hisaab, a concept app for exactly this problem.

What you'll find here:
• 5-minute guides to everyday student money situations
• Shorts with one habit each, in under 30 seconds
• The design and editing process behind the app and the videos

New videos every Sunday. Subscribe if your group chat has a "who owes whom" problem.
