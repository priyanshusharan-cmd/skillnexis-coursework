# 60-second educational video plan — How to make a strong password

**0–5s Hook:** “Could someone guess your password in a few tries?”
**5–15s:** Explain that reused passwords make one breach affect multiple accounts. On-screen text: “Don’t reuse passwords.”
**15–30s:** Show a password manager creating a long unique password. Blur all real credentials; use a demo screen.
**30–45s:** Turn on multi-factor authentication; show generic settings only.
**45–55s:** Recap: unique, long, stored safely, MFA enabled.
**55–60s CTA:** “Check your most important accounts today.”

## Shot list

1. Talking head, medium shot, quiet room. 2. Over-shoulder demo with fictional account. 3. Close-up checklist graphic. 4. Talking-head recap. Add captions, cut pauses, lower music under speech, normalize levels, and export 1080p H.264.
