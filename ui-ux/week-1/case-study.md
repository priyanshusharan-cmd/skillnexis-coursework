# Week 1 UI/UX case study — Food ordering app

## UI and UX

UI is the visible interface (type, color, controls); UX is the full experience of finding, ordering, paying, and receiving food. Design thinking moves through empathize, define, ideate, prototype, and test.

## Five app heuristics

- **Google Maps:** strong location context; dense maps can compete with task details.
- **Instagram:** familiar navigation; feed ranking can make the next action unclear.
- **Spotify:** persistent playback controls; large catalogs need clear hierarchy.
- **Amazon:** rich product detail; checkout should keep costs and delivery dates visible.
- **WhatsApp:** direct conversation model; settings and privacy controls should remain discoverable.

This is a heuristic exercise, not a current usability study with recruited participants.

## Persona and journey

**Maya**, 21, student, wants a reasonably priced meal delivered between classes. Journey: open app → choose address → browse → compare price/time → customize → pay → track. Pain points: hidden fees, unavailable items after checkout, unclear substitutions.
